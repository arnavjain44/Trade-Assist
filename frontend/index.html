5 EMA & VWAP — What this shows

The 5 EMA reacts quickly to recent price action, making it ideal for intraday trend detection. VWAP represents the true volume-weighted average price — the institutional benchmark. Price above both suggests buying pressure; below signals distribution.

RSI Momentum — What this shows

RSI measures the speed and change of price movements on a 0–100 scale. Readings above 70 indicate the stock is overbought — a potential pullback zone. Below 30 signals oversold conditions — a potential bounce zone. 40–60 is neutral momentum.

MACD Signal — What this shows

MACD shows the relationship between the 12-day and 26-day EMAs. When the MACD line crosses above the signal line, it generates a bullish signal — a potential entry opportunity. Crossing below is bearish — consider exiting or shorting.
